Privacy policy

Last updated 19 November 2026.

The Singleplayer At Play tool is used only by the owner of the Singleplayer At Play YouTube channel, on the owner's own computer, to manage that channel's videos.

It signs in to YouTube with the channel owner's permission and uses that access for two things: setting the thumbnail of the channel's own live streams, and editing the descriptions of the channel's own videos. The sign-in key is kept on the owner's computer.

The tool does not collect, store or share information about viewers or anyone else. It sends nothing to any service other than YouTube, and it has no advertising, analytics or tracking.

The channel owner can withdraw the tool's access at any time from the Google account's security settings, under third-party access.
