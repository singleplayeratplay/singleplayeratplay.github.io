Singleplayer At Play tools

Singleplayer At Play is a YouTube gaming channel: youtube.com/@Singleplayeratplay.

This site describes the small private tool the channel uses to look after its own videos. While a live stream is on air, the tool takes a still from the game being played and sets it as the stream's thumbnail, so the picture matches what is happening now. After a stream it can also update that video's description.

The tool works only on the Singleplayer At Play channel. It is not offered to anyone else and has no sign-up.
